WELCOME TO BKMI

Beauty skills taught with purpose.

BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School is a practical beauty training school in Yaba, Lagos. We help learners develop technical ability, neat work habits, hygiene, confidence and the customer-care mindset needed in the beauty industry.

Our classes combine theory, instructor demonstrations and supervised hands-on practice. Students are encouraged to build a portfolio and demonstrate the skills they have learned.
