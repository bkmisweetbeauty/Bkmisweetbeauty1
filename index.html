ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Welcome to BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School

BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School is a beauty training academy located in Yaba, Lagos. We provide practical training for individuals who want to develop professional beauty skills and build opportunities within the beauty industry.

Our training combines theory, demonstrations and supervised practical sessions. We encourage discipline, creativity, neatness, hygiene, confidence and excellent customer care.

Practical Training

Learn through demonstrations and supervised hands-on practice.

Professional Development

Develop the confidence and skills needed to serve clients professionally.

Business Awareness

Build an understanding of how to turn your skills into business opportunities.
