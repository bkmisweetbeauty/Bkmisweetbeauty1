ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Where Beauty Passion Becomes Professional Excellence!

Welcome to BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School, a Government-Approved beauty training academy located in Yaba, Lagos.

We transform passion into professional skills through practical, career-focused training designed to equip aspiring beauty professionals with the knowledge, confidence and hands-on experience to thrive in the beauty industry.

Why Choose BKMI-Sweetbeauty?

Our training combines classroom theory, professional demonstrations and supervised practical sessions, with a strong focus on:

Professional Skills and Creativity

Develop practical beauty skills, creativity and attention to detail.

Discipline and Confidence

Build the confidence and professional attitude needed to succeed.

Hygiene and Customer Care

Learn the importance of neatness, hygiene and excellent client service.

Business Awareness

Develop an entrepreneurial mindset and learn to turn your skills into opportunities.

Professional Training. Practical Experience. Endless Possibilities.

Whether you aspire to become a makeup artist, hairstylist, nail technician, cosmetologist, brow technician, esthetician or beauty entrepreneur, BKMI provides the opportunity to turn your passion into a valuable professional skill.

Don't just admire the beauty industry. Become part of it.
