BKMI-SWEETBEAUTY COSMETOLOGY SCHOOL · YABA, LAGOS

Diploma in Hairdressing in Yaba, Lagos

Develop professional hairdressing skills through BKMI-Sweetbeauty's six-month practical Diploma in Hairdressing programme in Yaba, Lagos. This course is suitable for beginners, aspiring hairstylists and professionals looking to upgrade their skills. Training combines theory, demonstrations and supervised hands-on practice.

Course duration6 months
Regular course fee₦500,000
Early-bird course fee₦450,000

Professional Hairdressing and Braiding

  • Weaving from scratch
  • Picture studying and recreation of weaving styles
  • Different braiding styles, including knotless, goddess, messy, traditional and butterfly braids
  • Twist braids and flat twist cornrows
  • Butterfly fly braids
  • Different braid parting patterns, including box, triangle, star and slant
  • Ghana weaving and waving cornrows
  • Feed-in cornrows
  • Stitch cornrows

Luxury Micro-Links Installation

  • Client consultations and appointment bookings
  • Training in four Micro-Links installation methods
  • Fundamentals of luxury Micro-Links placement
  • Hair assessment and selection of suitable installation methods
  • Micro-Links maintenance and removal
  • Preparing I-Tip extensions from weft hair
  • Styling and layering

Hair Styling, Treatment and Installation Techniques

  • Centre-part, fringe, side-part and round-closure hairstyles
  • Invisible parting techniques
  • Different crochet hairstyles
  • Faux locs, dreadlocks, butterfly locs and artificial dreadlocks installation
  • Skull caps, Ogo and pixie-cut hairstyles
  • Hair retouching, perming, relaxing and texturising
  • Hair washing, setting and styling
  • Natural hair treatments, straightening and styling
  • Installation without closures and frontals
  • Closure and frontal installation
  • 360 closure installation and wigging
  • Ponytail installation
  • Bridal hairstyling and hair fixing
  • Natural hair bridal styling
  • Traditional bridal hair installation and styling
  • Natural hair colouring and highlights

Professional Wig Making and Construction

  • Ventilation of closures, frontals and full lace wigs
  • Braided wigs in different styles
  • Ghana weaving wigs in different styles
  • Hand-sewn and machine-sewn wig construction
  • Head measurements and measurement transfer
  • Centre-part, fringe, U-part and other wig constructions
  • Skull-cap and pixie-cut wig construction

Business Seminar, Mentorship and Support

  • Free business seminar and guidance on getting started
  • Continuous mentorship and support
  • Business marketing strategies
  • Supplier contacts for beauty products and human hair vendors

Practical Training at BKMI-Sweetbeauty

Students learn through instructor demonstrations and supervised practical sessions. Classes run Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Students are expected to provide their own training materials, products and models where required.

Skills and Career Opportunities

This programme develops practical skills for careers in professional hairdressing, braiding, Micro-Links installation, wig making and bridal hairstyling. Students also receive business guidance to help them build and grow their own beauty businesses.

Why Study at BKMI-Sweetbeauty?

BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School combines theory, demonstrations and supervised practical training. We emphasise discipline, creativity, neatness, hygiene, customer care and professional skill development.
