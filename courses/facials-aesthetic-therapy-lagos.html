Facials & Aesthetic Therapy in Yaba, Lagos

Develop practical skincare and facial treatment skills through BKMI-Sweetbeauty's one-month Facials & Aesthetic Therapy programme in Yaba, Lagos. This course introduces students to basic and advanced facial care, skin analysis, professional skincare products and beauty treatment techniques through theory, demonstrations and supervised practical sessions.

Basic Facial Training

  • Skin analysis and understanding common skin conditions
  • Double cleansing and toning techniques
  • Basic facial care for acne-prone skin
  • Exfoliation methods and skincare principles
  • Facial masking and moisturizing
  • Brightening and glow facial treatments
  • Basic facial massage techniques
  • Skincare product knowledge and scientific ingredients

Advanced Facial Training

  • Advanced facial treatment principles
  • Hydra facial concepts and skincare techniques
  • High-frequency facial treatment principles
  • Anti-ageing facial care
  • Advanced facial massage techniques
  • Customised facial care for different skin concerns
  • Professional skincare assessment and treatment planning

Additional Beauty Training Modules

  • Hair waxing techniques
  • Vajacials – basic and advanced beauty care
  • Pedicure – basic and advanced techniques

Practical Training at BKMI-Sweetbeauty

Students learn through instructor demonstrations and supervised practical sessions. Training focuses on skincare knowledge, product selection, hygiene, client care and professional treatment techniques. Classes run Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Students are expected to provide their own training materials, products and models where required.

Skills and Career Opportunities

This programme helps students develop practical skills for beauty and skincare services. Career opportunities may include working as a facial therapist, skincare professional or beauty entrepreneur, subject to the qualifications and professional scope required for each service.

Why Study at BKMI-Sweetbeauty?

BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School combines theory, demonstrations and supervised practical learning. We emphasise hygiene, discipline, confidence, creativity, customer care and professional standards to help students develop useful beauty industry skills.
