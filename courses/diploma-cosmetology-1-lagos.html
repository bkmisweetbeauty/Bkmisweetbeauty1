Diploma in Cosmetology 1 in Yaba, Lagos

BKMI-Sweetbeauty Diploma in Cosmetology Course 1 is an extensive 11-month professional beauty training programme in Yaba, Lagos. It is designed for beginners, individuals upgrading their skills and experienced beauty professionals who want to expand their expertise. The programme combines theory, demonstrations and supervised practical training across multiple beauty disciplines. Students practise and demonstrate the techniques taught throughout the programme.

Comprehensive Cosmetology Course 1 Curriculum

The programme covers the following professional beauty subjects:

  • Hairdressing
  • Makeup Artistry and Bridal Makeup
  • Basic and Advanced Facials
  • Hair Waxing Techniques
  • Nail Technology – Beginner to Professional
  • Pedicure – Four Techniques
  • Braiding and Braided Wig Making
  • Hair Sew-in and Ponytail Styling
  • Artificial Dreadlocks, Soft Locs, Faux Locs, Butterfly Locs and related styles
  • Bridal Hairstyling – Western and Traditional
  • Wig Making – Hand and Machine Sewing Techniques
  • Closure and Frontal Ventilation
  • Frontal Installation and Customisation
  • Hair and Wig Laundry, Revamping, Bleaching, Colouring and Treatments
  • Micro-Links Installation – Four Techniques
  • Semi-Permanent Brows and Eyelashes
  • Brows and Eyelash Training, including Semi-Permanent Makeup
  • Colourimetry and Colour Theory
  • Lip Neutralisation and Lip Blush

Practical Training and Assessment

At BKMI-Sweetbeauty, students don't just learn the theory. They participate in demonstrations, supervised hands-on practice and practical assessments. Students are expected to practise and demonstrate the techniques covered in their training.

The programme is suitable for beginners, existing beauty practitioners seeking to upgrade their skills and professionals who want to expand their range of services.

Career and Business Opportunities

Graduates can use their acquired skills to pursue opportunities as professional hairdressers, makeup artists, bridal beauty specialists, wig makers, braiding specialists, nail technicians, micro-links technicians, brow and eyelash artists, facial therapists and beauty entrepreneurs. They can also develop independent beauty businesses, work as freelance practitioners or expand existing salon and beauty studio services, within their professional scope of practice.

Why Study at BKMI-Sweetbeauty?

Our training emphasises practical competence, creativity, neatness, hygiene, confidence, customer care and business awareness. We aim to help students develop useful professional skills for the beauty industry.

Training at BKMI combines theory, demonstrations and supervised practical sessions. Classes run Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Students are expected to provide their own training materials, products and models where required.
