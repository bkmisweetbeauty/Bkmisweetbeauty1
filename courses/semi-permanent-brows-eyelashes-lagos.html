Semi-Permanent Brows and Eyelashes in Yaba, Lagos

Develop professional beauty skills through BKMI-Sweetbeauty's six-week intensive Semi-Permanent Brows and Eyelash Course in Yaba, Lagos. This practical programme introduces students to brow techniques, eyelash extensions and lip enhancement, with demonstrations and supervised live-model practice.

  • Microblading techniques

  • Lip blush techniques

Live Model Practice, Training Tools and Mentorship

The training includes practical sessions on live models, training tools and mentorship. Students develop their skills through instructor demonstrations and supervised practice, with emphasis on hygiene, infection prevention, client consultation, consent and appropriate aftercare.

Practical Training at BKMI-Sweetbeauty

Classes run Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Students are expected to provide their own training materials and products, except for tools specifically included in the course.

Skills and Career Opportunities

This programme helps students develop skills in brow artistry, eyelash extensions and lip enhancement. Graduates can explore opportunities in beauty studios, salons and independent beauty businesses, subject to applicable professional and safety requirements.

Why Study at BKMI-Sweetbeauty?

BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School combines theory, demonstrations and supervised practical training. We emphasise discipline, creativity, neatness, hygiene, customer care and professional skill development.
