Professional Wig Training in Yaba, Lagos

Build professional wig-making skills through BKMI-Sweetbeauty's six-week, 100% practical Professional Wig Training Course in Yaba, Lagos. This programme is suitable for beginners and anyone looking to improve their wig-making, customization and styling skills. Students learn through demonstrations and supervised hands-on practice.

100% Practical Wig Training at BKMI-Sweetbeauty

Students learn through demonstrations and supervised hands-on practice. Classes run Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Students are expected to provide their own training materials, products and models where required.

Skills and Career Opportunities

This course develops practical skills for starting or growing a career in wig making, wig customization, frontal installation and professional wig styling. Graduates can explore freelance work, salon opportunities and their own wig-making businesses.

Why Study at BKMI-Sweetbeauty?

BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School combines practical demonstrations with supervised training. We emphasise creativity, neatness, discipline, hygiene and professional skill development.
