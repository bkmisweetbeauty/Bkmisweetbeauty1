BKMI-SWEETBEAUTY COSMETOLOGY SCHOOL · YABA, LAGOS

Barbering & Natural Dreadlocks in Yaba, Lagos

Learn professional barbering, natural dreadlocks and hair colouring techniques through BKMI-Sweetbeauty's two-month practical training programme in Yaba, Lagos. This course is suitable for beginners and individuals looking to develop or upgrade their barbering and hair grooming skills. Training combines theory, demonstrations and supervised hands-on practice.

Course duration2 months

Barbering Course Curriculum

  • Barbering tools, equipment and hygiene
  • Hair and scalp analysis
  • Clipper, trimmer and razor handling
  • Basic and advanced haircut techniques
  • Fades, tapers and blending
  • Beard shaping, trimming and grooming
  • Hairline and neckline detailing
  • Contemporary men's hairstyles
  • Finishing, styling and aftercare

Natural Dreadlocks Training

  • Introduction to natural dreadlocks
  • Hair and scalp assessment
  • Sectioning and dreadlock formation
  • Starter loc techniques
  • Palm rolling and maintenance
  • Loc repair, tightening and retwisting
  • Loc styling and creative finishing
  • Loc cleansing, hygiene and aftercare

Hair Tinting and Bleaching Training

  • Hair and scalp assessment
  • Colour theory and shade selection
  • Hair colour products and tools
  • Tinting and root application
  • Full-colour application
  • Bleaching and lightening techniques
  • Highlights, lowlights and creative colour
  • Toners and colour correction
  • Colour safety, aftercare and maintenance

Practical Training at BKMI-Sweetbeauty

Students learn through instructor demonstrations and supervised practical sessions covering barbering, dreadlock techniques and hair colouring. Classes run Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Students are expected to provide their own training materials, products and models where required.

Skills and Career Opportunities

This programme develops practical skills for careers as professional barbers, natural dreadlocks specialists and hair colouring professionals. Students can explore opportunities in barbershops, salons, freelance grooming services and their own beauty businesses.

Why Study at BKMI-Sweetbeauty?

BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School combines theory, demonstrations and supervised practical training. We emphasise discipline, creativity, neatness, hygiene, customer care and professional skill development.
