Nail Technology in Yaba, Lagos

Develop professional nail technology skills through BKMI-Sweetbeauty's two-month practical nail technician training programme in Yaba, Lagos. This course is designed for beginners, aspiring nail technicians and individuals looking to upgrade their existing nail artistry skills. Students learn nail preparation, extensions, enhancements, creative designs and professional nail business skills through demonstrations and supervised practical training.

Nail Technology Course Curriculum

Nail Fundamentals and Preparation

  • Nail anatomy and hygiene
  • Nail tools and product knowledge
  • Nail tips, sizing and extensions
  • Nail filing and shaping techniques
  • Gel polish application
  • Toenail application and design

Professional Nail Extensions and Enhancements

  • Gel-X application
  • Builder gel and hard gel techniques
  • Polygel application and refills
  • Acrylic nail application, extensions and refills
  • Encapsulation techniques
  • Ombre acrylic application
  • Acrylic toenails

Nail Art and Creative Designs

  • Ombre, marble and French nail designs
  • Trending nail art techniques
  • Three-dimensional (3D) nail designs
  • Chrome effects, stones and foils

Nail Business and Professional Development

  • Nail business setup and management
  • Pricing nail services
  • Client communication and customer care
  • Content creation for nail businesses

Bonus Training

  • Stick-on and press-on nail techniques
  • Nail photography
  • Mentorship

Practical Nail Technician Training at BKMI

Training combines theory, instructor demonstrations and supervised hands-on practice. Students have the opportunity to practise the techniques covered in the curriculum. Classes run Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Students are expected to provide their own training materials, products and models where required.

Skills and Career Opportunities

Students develop practical skills in nail preparation, gel application, acrylic extensions, nail enhancements, creative nail art, client service and business management. These skills can support opportunities as professional nail technicians, freelance nail artists, salon professionals and nail business owners.

Why Study Nail Technology at BKMI-Sweetbeauty?

BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School focuses on practical competence, creativity, neatness, hygiene and professional customer care. Our training helps students develop the technical and business skills needed to build a career in the nail industry.
