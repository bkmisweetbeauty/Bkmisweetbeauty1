Professional Braids Course in Yaba, Lagos

The Professional Braiding Course at BKMI-Sweetbeauty Cosmetology School in Yaba, Lagos is a practical 3-month training programme designed to develop professional braiding and braided wig-making skills. Students learn different traditional and contemporary braiding techniques, attachment methods, braid styling, colour mixing and wig construction. Training combines demonstrations with supervised hands-on practice.

Professional Braiding Course Curriculum

Students receive practical training in a variety of professional braiding techniques, including:

  • Weaving from scratch
  • Different cornrow styles
  • Box braids
  • Knotless braids
  • Twist braids
  • Flat twist cornrows
  • Micro-twist braids
  • Goddess braids
  • Watermelon braids
  • Bob braids
  • Stitch cornrow braids
  • Feed-in braids
  • Invisible Ghana weaving cornrows
  • Messy braids
  • Different Ghana weaving styles
  • Hair attachment techniques
  • Curling and styling different braids
  • Hair extension colour mixing

Professional Braided Wig Making Class

The programme also includes specialised training in braided wig construction and finishing techniques.

  • Making Ghana weaving wigs with and without closures and frontals
  • Making braided wigs with and without closures and frontals
  • Curling and styling braided wigs
  • Bleaching and tinting wig knots

Practical Training and Skill Development

Students learn through instructor demonstrations, supervised practical sessions and hands-on exercises. The training covers both braid creation and the construction of finished braided wigs, helping students develop practical skills they can apply in professional beauty settings.

Classes run Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Students are expected to provide their own training materials, products and models where required.

Career and Business Opportunities

After completing the programme, graduates can pursue opportunities as professional hair braiders, cornrow and Ghana weaving specialists, knotless braid stylists, twist braid specialists and braided wig makers. They can offer freelance braiding services, work with salons and beauty studios, establish their own braiding businesses or expand existing hair and wig businesses.

Why Study Braiding at BKMI-Sweetbeauty?

Our training emphasises practical competence, creativity, neatness, hygiene, finishing techniques and customer care. Students are encouraged to develop the confidence and technical ability needed to provide professional braiding and braided wig services.
